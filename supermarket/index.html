About Us

Opened on 12 April 2007, Mulamoottil Super Market has been part of daily life in Puthuppally for 18 years.

M.K. Thomas (Pappachan Mulamoottil) founded the store. For the past three years his son, Shajan K. Thomas, has led it, keeping the same care for quality products and straightforward service.

A steady team keeps the aisles friendly and the visit unhurried. The shop is built around a homely welcome — familiar shelves, and the things a household needs close to home.
